# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Tarvos a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behavior that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, experiences, and viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them openly

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any private communication initiated in the project's name)
and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately via GitHub's **Report abuse** tool or
by opening a private Security Advisory on this repository (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the mechanism). Maintainers will review the
report, keep it confidential as far as practicable, and respond with an
appropriate action — a warning, a temporary or permanent ban from project
spaces, or referral to the platform where the behavior occurred.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith
may face temporary or permanent consequences as determined by the other
maintainers of the project.
